## Diagnostics and imaging

- **BMJ editorial on non-invasive tests** — PubMed has no abstract, and
  Europe PMC did not provide an open full text. The public article page
  shows the opening and then a subscription wall. From that opening: the
  standfirst is "NICE recommendations may not meet clinical needs." The
  authors say public debate has described delays averaging nine years
  from symptom onset, that draft NICE guidance now recommends Endotest,
  and that a committee meeting is upcoming and there are concerns whether
  the evidence justifies the recommendation. They also say that, despite
  extensive research, no simple test of molecules in blood, urine, or
  saliva has proved consistently accurate across populations. The rest of
  the editorial was not read, so it is not summarized. The "nine years"
  sentence describes public debate. It is not a result of this editorial
  and is not adopted. The WHO fact-sheet range of 4–12 years stands.
  [PMID 43078525](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43078525/) — *why it matters:* the visible opening questions a draft
  recommendation. It does not supply an accuracy figure, and it does not
  move the saliva-diagnostics page, which already treats Endotest as
  emerging and not an established standard.
- **Non-invasive omics and machine learning** — critical narrative
  review. The authors separate formal multi-omics integration from
  single-omics models, multi-analyte panels, and multi-site profiling.
  They describe the most mature evidence as a multicenter blood-based
  model combining microRNAs, proteins, a steroid hormone, and clinical
  variables: an independent retrospective cohort had an area under the
  curve of 0.944, sensitivity 80.0%, and specificity 97.5%, and they say
  prospective intended-use validation and independent replication are
  still necessary. A serum proteome-metabolome model did not outperform
  a metabolite-only panel for endometriosis versus healthy controls.
  Stool, menstrual-cell, cell-free DNA, urinary microRNA, and salivary
  microRNA studies are described as discovery-stage or single-omics. The
  authors say no current assay has demonstrated the analytical validity,
  external clinical validity, clinical utility, and interlaboratory
  reproducibility needed to replace established pathways.
  [PMID 43095119](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43095119/) — *why it matters:* a review of other studies, not a new
  locked test. The blood-model figures are not adopted as a standard.
  Salivary microRNA stays discovery-stage. The saliva-diagnostics page
  is unchanged. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **Gene-expression signatures** — narrative review. The authors say
  signatures from surgically collected lesions must be distinguished
  from markers that can be measured in minimally invasive specimens.
  They name AEBP1, KLF2, HOXB6, and RORB as individual candidates with
  promising discriminatory performance, and say HOXB6 may also help
  separate stages. They say most proposed biomarkers remain at the
  candidate stage, and that heterogeneity limits comparison across
  studies.
  [PMID 43091718](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43091718/) — *why it matters:* candidate genes, not a diagnostic
  panel. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **Nomogram for ovarian endometrioma among people who already have
  endometriosis** — retrospective, one hospital, January 2021–December
  2025. 342 patients with pathologically confirmed endometriosis: 103
  with ovarian endometrioma and 239 with other types, split 7:3 into
  training and validation. Independent correlates were a history of
  dysmenorrhea (OR 60.44, 95% CI 20.95–213.90), infertility (OR 13.10,
  95% CI 4.85–40.21), fibrinogen (OR 1.84, 95% CI 1.13–3.01), and
  lymphocyte count (OR 0.42, 95% CI 0.16–0.99). Area under the curve was
  0.945 in training and 0.948 in validation. The authors say the model
  does not predict future endometrioma and needs prospective validation.
  [PMID 43079448](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43079448/) — *why it matters:* a classifier inside a group that
  already has a tissue diagnosis. The intervals are very wide. It is not
  a test for people whose diagnosis is still open. Indexed in the edat
  supplement.
- **Portable electroviscerography** — prospective pilot at two tertiary
  centres in São Paulo. 86 women aged 18–45: 48 with endometriosis
  confirmed by histology or imaging, and 38 followed for other benign
  gynecologic conditions. Abdominal-wall recordings of gastrointestinal
  myoelectric activity were compared with laparoscopy plus histology or
  with transvaginal ultrasound after bowel preparation. Against
  laparoscopy, sensitivity was 93.75% and positive predictive value
  88.24%; specificity could not be fully estimated because few
  surgically disease-free cases were available. Against ultrasound,
  sensitivity was 81.25%, specificity 21.05%, positive predictive value
  56.52%, and negative predictive value 47.06%. One results sentence
  suggests a triage tool for excluding disease. The conclusion says the
  performance does not support use as a standalone diagnostic or
  rule-out test.
  [PMID 43077756](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43077756/) — *why it matters:* high sensitivity with low
  specificity, and a negative predictive value near one half, which does
  not exclude disease. Not a scanning protocol. Indexed in the edat
  supplement.
- **KL and TLE1 tissue signature** — four transcriptomic datasets, with
  machine-learning selection of two anoikis-related genes. Both were
  downregulated in endometriosis tissue. Training areas under the curve
  were 0.952 for KL and 0.973 for TLE1. A two-gene logistic model had
  an area under the curve of 1.000 in GSE7307, GSE7305, and GSE23339,
  and 0.817 in GSE51981. qPCR and Western blot in an independent set
  used 3 samples per group. The authors say this is a tissue analysis
  and that clinical use would need larger prospective cohorts and
  non-invasive samples.
  [PMID 43072262](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43072262/) — *why it matters:* reused tissue datasets, including
  perfect areas under the curve. Not a blood or saliva test. Indexed in
  the edat supplement.
- **Clinical machine-learning models at one hospital** — retrospective,
  one tertiary hospital in China, women aged 18 and older, random 7:3
  split. 233 patients, 120 with endometriosis. Independent factors were
  a history of uterine fibroids, menopausal status, dysmenorrhea, white
  blood cell count, CA125, and CA199. On the validation split, area
  under the curve, sensitivity, and specificity were 0.878 (95% CI
  0.793–0.962), 0.944, and 0.706 for logistic regression; 0.878 (95% CI
  0.795–0.960), 0.694, and 0.912 for random forest; 0.853 (95% CI
  0.764–0.941), 0.667, and 0.853 for a support vector machine; and 0.860
  (95% CI 0.777–0.943), 0.722, and 0.794 for XGBoost. The authors say
  the sample is limited and the split is internal, so caution is still
  warranted.
  [PMID 43065352](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43065352/) — *why it matters:* one centre, no external test.
  Not a diagnostic standard. Indexed in the edat supplement.

## Surgery

- **Inguinal endometrioma mistaken for a hernia** — one case. A
  34-year-old woman with a history of Hodgkin lymphoma had a fluctuating
  left groin mass. Ultrasound showed a 2.2 × 0.6 × 1.9 cm hypoechoic
  lesion. Excision found a lobulated cystic mass herniating through the
  indirect inguinal canal. Pathology was fibroadipose tissue with foci
  of endometriosis.
  [PMID 43098182](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43098182/) — *why it matters:* a single operation. The
  abstract's "approximately 5–10%" and "less than 1%" sentences are
  background, not counts from this case. It is not a rule for groin
  masses.
- **Pregnancy after fertility-preserving surgery, with and without bowel
  involvement** — retrospective cohort at one tertiary centre, 2017–2024.
  202 women aged 18–45 with histologically confirmed deep endometriosis
  who wished to conceive: 127 without bowel involvement and 75 with it.
  140 became pregnant and 119 had a live birth. Pregnancy was 64.0% with
  bowel involvement and 72.4% without (adjusted HR 0.88, 95% CI
  0.60–1.28). Live birth was 50.7% and 63.8% (adjusted HR 0.87, 95% CI
  0.57–1.32). Bowel involvement was associated with fewer spontaneous
  pregnancies (adjusted subdistribution HR 0.53, 95% CI 0.30–0.94) and
  more medically assisted pregnancies (adjusted subdistribution HR 1.72,
  95% CI 1.01–2.92). The authors say the pregnancy and live-birth
  estimates are not precise enough to exclude a clinically meaningful
  difference.
  [PMID 43090964](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43090964/) — *why it matters:* one centre's experience after
  surgery in women who wanted a pregnancy. It does not choose an
  operation. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **Quality of life six months after surgery plus a postoperative
  hormone** — prospective cohort. 50 women with histologically confirmed
  rASRM stage III–IV disease had surgery, then dienogest, a GnRH
  agonist, or ethinylestradiol/dienogest. All eight SF-36v2 domains
  improved (all p<0.001). Median numerical pain fell from 8 (IQR 7–8)
  to 2 (IQR 1–3) (p<0.001). Pain reduction was associated with the
  quality-of-life change; four of eight correlations remained
  significant after Holm correction, strongest for bodily pain
  (ρ=0.538). No statistically significant difference in pain or
  quality-of-life change was detected by stage or by which hormone was
  used. Hot flushes differed across hormone groups and were most
  frequent with a GnRH agonist (56.3%; Holm-adjusted p=0.010). Allocation
  was not randomized.
  [PMID 43090905](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43090905/) — *why it matters:* scores moved after surgery
  and a hormone in one observational series. The authors say this does
  not establish that the hormone regimens are equivalent. It is not a
  comparison with no surgery, and it does not set a postoperative
  prescription. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **Endometriosis at a previous trocar site** — six women with
  histologically confirmed abdominal-wall endometriosis after
  gynecologic laparoscopy, screened from admissions between January 2018
  and March 2025. Median age at diagnosis was 41 years (range 36–48).
  Median time from the index surgery to symptom onset was 15.5 months
  (range 2–108). All had cyclic pain at a previous trocar site; four had
  a palpable mass; one had cyclic umbilical bleeding. All lesions were
  excised. One patient had a local recurrence, four remained
  recurrence-free, and one was lost to follow-up. Weak stromal FHL1
  staining and absent stromal PI16 staining were described as an
  exploratory pattern that needs a larger comparison.
  [PMID 43075461](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43075461/) — *why it matters:* six cases. Not a port-site
  prevention protocol. Indexed in the edat supplement.

## Mechanisms and biology

- **Multiancestry genome-wide association study** — Global Biobank
  Meta-analysis Initiative, over 1 million participants, 32% of
  non-European ancestry. 58 genomic regions, 27 previously unreported,
  including loci described as unique to African and admixed American
  groups, plus new procedure-confirmed case definitions. Genetically
  driven comorbidity searches found dozens of associations. Multiomic
  analyses implicated 314 genes. The authors say these suggest multiple
  targets.
  [PMID 43064779](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43064779/) — *why it matters:* a genetic map. It is not a
  clinical test, and a suggested target is not an OpenEndo repurposing
  verdict.
- **GLP-1 receptor signaling as a hypothesis** — narrative review. The
  authors propose endometriosis as an adaptive ecosystem and discuss
  GLP-1 receptor signaling as a candidate link to metabolism,
  macrophages, vessels, fibrosis, neuroimmune signals, and mitochondria.
  They say effects of GLP-1 receptor activation outside endometriosis
  have not been shown in lesions, that human evidence is limited to
  observational peritoneal-fluid concentrations, and that direct evidence
  of a causal role or of therapeutic efficacy is lacking.
  [PMID 43097232](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43097232/) — *why it matters:* a hypothesis paper. It is
  not evidence that a GLP-1 receptor agonist treats endometriosis, and
  it is not an M3 verdict.
- **Immune and inflammatory mediators** — narrative review of
  interleukins, chemokines, tumor necrosis factor, interferons, growth
  factors, macrophage migration inhibitory factor, and of macrophages, T
  cells, and natural killer cells in lesion survival. The abstract's
  "approximately 10–15%" sentence is background, not a new prevalence.
  The title's "new perspectives for diagnosis and treatment" does not
  name a test or a drug in the abstract.
  [PMID 43092120](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43092120/) — *why it matters:* a mechanism summary. No
  diagnostic cutoff and no treatment result are reported.
- **Macrophage-reprogramming molecule in mice** — two induced-endometriosis
  models in female FVB/N mice. RRx-001 was associated with less lesion
  growth and less pain-related response. Peritoneal macrophages formed
  seven subpopulations; treatment was associated with fewer
  disease-modified populations. Transcriptional changes were described
  as largely limited to the peritoneal cavity and blood, with little
  change in lung, liver, spleen, and endometrium. Preliminary studies
  did not show a change in estrous cycling or fertility. The authors say
  causality and translational relevance are not established. Funding
  included an MRC iCASE studentship with Ferring as the industrial
  partner.
  [PMID 43084987](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43084987/) — *why it matters:* a mouse experiment. RRx-001
  is not an OpenEndo repurposing candidate and not a human treatment.
- **Interleukin-10** — narrative review. Circulating IL-10 is described
  as inconsistent; peritoneal-fluid and lesion measurements track local
  immune organization more closely. The authors say macrophage states
  are not a simple M1/M2 split, and that preclinical evidence does not
  support indiscriminate systemic IL-10 blockade or supplementation.
  [PMID 43071277](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43071277/) — *why it matters:* IL-10 is not a biomarker to
  order and not a drug to give or block.
- **CX3CR1 knockout in mice** — experimental endometriosis, 7 weeks.
  Wild-type n=8 and CX3CR1 knockout n=7, C57BL/6. Knockout lesions were
  smaller, with fewer CD163-positive cells per mm² and lower CD163 mRNA.
  The authors call this the first in vivo functional evidence for this
  axis and a possible non-hormonal target.
  [PMID 43064688](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43064688/) — *why it matters:* 15 mice. A suggested target
  is not a drug.
- **A traditional formula and ferroptosis in rats** — Neiyi prescription
  of QIU, given at 6.3, 12.5, and 25 g/kg, with a p53 activator, a p53
  inhibitor, a ferroptosis inhibitor (ferrostatin-1), and an inducer
  (erastin). The formula and the p53 activator were associated with
  smaller lesions, lower glutathione, lower SLC7A11 and GPX4, higher
  p53, PTGS2, and NOX1, and higher malondialdehyde, ferrous iron, and
  reactive oxygen species. Ferrostatin-1 and the p53 inhibitor worsened
  the model and opposed the formula. The abstract says molecular docking
  predicted that icariin may interact with p53.
  [PMID 43062770](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43062770/) — *why it matters:* a rat experiment in the same
  direction already stated on the public explainer: in that lesion
  model, pushing ferroptosis was associated with smaller lesions. It is
  not a medicine, it is not sulfasalazine, and the lesion-cell sentence
  is unchanged.
- **Ferroptosis-related transcripts in endometriosis-associated ovarian
  cancer** — multi-cohort transcriptomic study. In GSE157153, 1,809
  detectable FerrDb genes were scored. The global score varied only
  modestly across pathological states, while 929 state-associated genes
  formed four programs. Network analysis prioritized CDK1, ERBB2, KRAS,
  PIK3CA, PTEN, JAK2, and SRC. The authors say this does not establish
  ferroptosis-specific functional activity.
  [PMID 43087691](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43087691/) — *why it matters:* expression programs in
  cancer-associated states, not a functional direction for ordinary
  lesions, and not a change to the sulfasalazine watchlist. Indexed in
  the edat supplement.
- **Liriodendrin in a mouse model** — intraperitoneal implantation of
  endometrial fragments. Liriodendrin was associated with smaller, lighter
  lesions and less thermal hyperalgesia, with lower NGF, TRPV1, CGRP,
  and IB4. The authors report PPARγ/RXRα/NRF2 activation and less NLRP3,
  GSDMD, IL-1β, and IL-18. The abstract does not give the dose or the
  number of animals.
  [PMID 42771953](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42771953/) — *why it matters:* a preclinical compound. It
  is not a supplement and not an M3 candidate.
- **Pain mechanisms** — narrative review in Endocrine Reviews. The
  authors describe pain as poorly correlated with lesion burden and as
  the sum of endocrine, immune, neuroangiogenic, and neural-plasticity
  signals, with estrogen dominance and progesterone resistance as
  upstream drivers. They propose a precision framework using mechanistic
  biomarkers and pain-specific endpoints. The "approximately 10%"
  sentence is background. "Delayed diagnosis" is stated without a new
  interval.
  [PMID 42267663](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42267663/) — *why it matters:* a mechanism review. The
  proposed framework is not a treatment protocol, and no delay figure is
  adopted.
- **HNRNPC and decidualization** — cell and mouse experiments. HNRNPC was
  upregulated in endometriosis and, through an m6A-associated increase in
  NEDD4 and ubiquitination of ANXA1, promoted M1 macrophage polarization
  and impaired stromal decidualization. Knockdown in pregnant model mice
  was associated with less decidualization impairment and a restored
  embryo count.
  [PMID 43096702](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43096702/) — *why it matters:* a mouse and cell axis. Not a
  fertility treatment. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **Anti-VEGFA siRNA nanoparticles in rats** — ternary peptide polyplexes
  given into lesions in a surgically induced model in female Wistar rats.
  Intralesional anti-VEGFA siRNA particles decorated with a cyclic RGD
  ligand were associated with smaller lesions, lower VEGFA expression,
  and an apparent reduction of CD34 staining in representative sections,
  compared with ligand-free particles.
  [PMID 43093709](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43093709/) — *why it matters:* intralesional delivery in
  rats. Not a human drug. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **Boron, curcumin, and piperine on eutopic cells** — primary cultures
  from four women with surgically and histologically confirmed
  endometriosis and four women without it. At 48 hours, approximate IC50
  values were about twofold higher in control cells than in
  endometriosis cells (glandular 321.3 ± 89.6 versus 160.4 ± 40.1 μg/mL;
  stromal 351.8 ± 59.4 versus 179.0 ± 29.9 μg/mL). Mann-Whitney p=0.029
  is the smallest p the authors say is attainable with four donors per
  group. Apoptosis rose in treated endometriosis cells (glandular
  54.5 ± 17.2% versus 8.7 ± 4.9%; stromal 55.2 ± 8.0% versus
  8.4 ± 6.5%). The cells were eutopic, not lesion cells.
  [PMID 43093352](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43093352/) — *why it matters:* an in vitro combination in
  eight donors. It is not a supplement. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **Wnt/β-catenin** — narrative review. The authors say excessive Wnt
  signaling may raise proliferation, inflammation, and fibrosis, and that
  inhibition reduced lesion burden and fibrosis in preclinical models.
  They point to oncology and fibrosis drugs as a translational frame.
  [PMID 43091398](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43091398/) — *why it matters:* a preclinical pathway. Not a
  Wnt inhibitor to use for endometriosis. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **JUNB in eutopic endometrium** — cross-sectional, one university
  hospital. 20 women with endometriosis and 25 without, sampled at
  laparoscopy. Most patients in both groups were taking hormones. EGR1,
  FOS, and FOSB did not differ. JUNB expression was higher in the control
  group (p=0.0417).
  [PMID 43088494](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43088494/) — *why it matters:* one gene differed in a small
  eutopic sample, most of whom were on hormones. Not a test. Indexed in
  the edat supplement.
- **Amphiregulin and epiregulin in endometrioma** — pilot case-control,
  28 women at cystectomy: 14 endometriomas and 14 other benign ovarian
  cysts. Median tissue AREG mRNA was 11.74 versus 0.83 (q<0.001) and
  EREG 1.95 versus 0.82 (q=0.0105). CA125 was 50.30 versus 17.70
  (q=0.0058). Serum EREG did not differ (q=0.2855). Serum AREG was lower
  in the endometrioma group only in a sensitivity analysis that assigned
  four right-censored values to 1000 pg/mL (127.26 versus 296.02 pg/mL;
  q=0.0149). Apparent tissue areas under the curve were 0.992 and 0.827.
  The authors say the sample, tissue mix, and lack of independent
  validation limit any diagnostic reading.
  [PMID 43087894](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43087894/) — *why it matters:* a 14-versus-14 tissue
  difference. The serum result depends on how censored values were
  filled. Not a blood test. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **MicroRNAs in endometrioma fluid** — exploratory microarray of
  uncentrifuged cyst fluid, n=7, endometrioma versus functional cyst.
  Principal component 1 accounted for 93.5% of variance. 37 features
  passed false-discovery and fold-change filters, including 35 mature
  microRNAs. Target networks were built in silico.
  [PMID 43089398](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43089398/) — *why it matters:* seven samples. Not a
  biomarker. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **Macrophages and a computational pain score** — reanalysis of
  GSE179640, 103,434 cells from 31 biopsies of 14 patients, using a
  60-gene literature module as a stand-in for pain because the public
  data have no pain scores. Macrophages were 68.8% of the high-score
  cells. After correction across two bulk cohorts, only TYROBP and
  CXCL12 remained significant. Spatial co-expression was unstable. The
  authors say the score is a computational surrogate.
  [PMID 43087839](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43087839/) — *why it matters:* a hypothesis from a gene
  list, not from measured pain. Not a pain test. Indexed in the edat
  supplement.
- **Pathophysiology review** — narrative review of adhesion, invasion,
  immune clearance, hypoxia, metabolism, steroid signaling, angiogenesis,
  and lymphangiogenesis, and of organoid models. The "approximately 10%"
  sentence is background. The authors say current hormonal and surgical
  treatments are not curative. They do not report a new trial.
  [PMID 43065415](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43065415/) — *why it matters:* a framework. Not a change
  in treatment. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **Computational repurposing** — six transcriptomic datasets, 135 shared
  differentially expressed genes, 11 hub proteins, and 10 proteins from
  LASSO. On a held-out test set the LASSO model had an area under the
  curve of 0.903, accuracy 87.8%, sensitivity 84.6%, and specificity
  93.3%. GSK-429286A and lopinavir had the highest predicted binding to
  PTGIS among ten prospective compounds. The authors also name C1QA,
  JAM3, POLRMT, STAB1, TAGLN, and TUFM as biomarker candidates.
  [PMID 43075688](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43075688/) — *why it matters:* docking and a reused
  expression model. Lopinavir and GSK-429286A are not OpenEndo M3
  candidates, and this paper does not change sirolimus, sulfasalazine,
  or any other verdict. Indexed in the edat supplement.

## Epidemiology and burden

- **Allergic disease and later endometriosis in Ontario** —
  population-based cohort of hospital-born females, 1988–1998,
  N=525,326, followed from the 10th birthday to death, loss of insurance,
  or December 2023 (median follow-up 19.7 years). Asthma, allergic
  rhinitis, and atopic dermatitis were time-varying exposures.
  Endometriosis incidence was 10.5 per 10,000 person-years with allergic
  disease and 6.4 per 10,000 without it. Adjusted hazard ratios were
  1.51 (95% CI 1.29–1.77) for allergic disease diagnosed by age 10, 1.35
  (95% CI 1.26–1.45) at age 20, and 1.21 (95% CI 1.13–1.29) at age 30.
  [PMID 43098290](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43098290/) — *why it matters:* an association in
  administrative records. It is not evidence that treating allergy
  prevents endometriosis, and it is not a screening program.
- **Time to first hospital diagnosis in young French women** —
  population-based cohort in the French National Health Data System.
  9,088 women aged 18–25 received a first hospital diagnosis of
  endometriosis in 2018–2021, an incidence of 7.5 per 10,000
  person-years. Onset was defined as the first time two healthcare
  events fell inside six months, looking back up to 10 years, and delay
  was the time from that proxy to the hospital diagnosis. The median was
  8.9 years (IQR 6.9–9.8), in a similar range by age at diagnosis and by
  endometriosis type. Most first consultations were with a general
  practitioner. A later first MRI prescription was associated with a
  longer interval.
  [PMID 43078066](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43078066/) — *why it matters:* a constructed interval to
  hospital diagnosis in one age band, not a patient-reported
  symptom-to-diagnosis mean for all ages. The 8.9-year median is not
  adopted. The WHO 4–12 year range stands.
- **Hormonal prescribing in a Japanese claims database** — women under
  40 who newly started a low-dose estrogen-progestin or dienogest for
  dysmenorrhea or endometriosis, January–June 2023, followed 12 months.
  16,176 patients. Initial regimens were ultra-low-dose fourth-generation
  combinations 44.2%, ultra-low-dose first-generation 20.8%, low-dose
  14.4%, dienogest 1 mg/day 9.0%, ultra-low-dose second-generation 4.4%,
  and dienogest 2 mg/day 7.1%. A single-regimen pattern was seen in
  84.3%. Mean proportion of days covered was 64.8% ± 34.2%, and 23.6%
  had coverage below 30%. The authors say claims data cannot tell
  discontinuation from adverse effects or from poor adherence. The
  abstract does not separate dysmenorrhea from endometriosis.
  [PMID 43084475](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43084475/) — *why it matters:* a prescribing snapshot for
  a mixed indication. It is not a regimen, and it does not change the
  Yselty (linzagolix) page.
- **Donor-sperm insemination and live birth** — retrospective cohort,
  November 2021–September 2024, women under 45 with confirmed tubal
  patency. 541 women, 1,497 cycles: 94 with endometriosis and 447
  without. Cumulative live birth per woman was 20.2% versus 32.2%
  (P=0.021). Live birth per cycle was 7.4% versus 11.6% (P=0.048). Early
  miscarriage was 29.6% versus 12.7% (P=0.022). After adjustment,
  endometriosis remained associated with lower odds of live birth
  (adjusted OR 0.5, 95% CI 0.2–0.9). The authors say a substantial
  proportion still had a live birth and that insemination can remain a
  first-line option with individual counselling.
  [PMID 43072032](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43072032/) — *why it matters:* an observational
  association. It does not set an insemination protocol. Indexed in the
  edat supplement.
- **Stigma and care-seeking** — qualitative interviews in a German
  sample of recently diagnosed participants. The abstract says repeated
  stigma from healthcare professionals eroded trust, led some people to
  withdraw from care, and fostered hyper-independence, and that a
  diagnosis both validated and distressed. The abstract does not give
  the number of interviews.
  [PMID 43095267](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43095267/) — *why it matters:* an interview study. It is
  not a measure of how often stigma occurs, and it is not a service
  specification. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **French EHP-5 questionnaire** — online survey, 1,439 French-speaking
  women with a medical diagnosis of endometriosis. The five core items
  had Cronbach's alpha 0.71 and McDonald's omega 0.71. A one-factor
  model had CFI 0.956, TLI 0.912, and SRMR 0.047; RMSEA was 0.111, above
  the usual cutoff. Scores were associated with pain intensity, pain
  frequency, fatigue, and constant pelvic pain. Measurement invariance
  was generally supported across infertility, parenthood, and
  comorbidity groups.
  [PMID 43068429](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43068429/) — *why it matters:* properties of a short
  quality-of-life form. It is not a new outcome that trials must use,
  and it is not a diagnostic test.
- **Follicular leucine-rich α-2 glycoprotein** — retrospective, oocyte
  retrieval 2020–2022. 18 women with endometriosis and 26 without.
  Serum LRG was 59.5 ± 24.7 versus 58.7 ± 15.3 μg/mL and follicular LRG
  39.3 ± 15.0 versus 42.9 ± 6.7 μg/mL, with no significant difference,
  and no significant difference by pregnancy. Follicular LRG correlated
  with serum anti-Müllerian hormone only in the endometriosis group
  (r=−0.47, p=0.047).
  [PMID 43082027](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43082027/) — *why it matters:* a small correlation. LRG
  is not adopted as an ovarian-reserve or IVF marker. Indexed in the
  edat supplement.
- **Bibliometric map of pain research** — 728 publications, 2006–2026,
  from PubMed and Web of Science. The authors describe rising output,
  with Italy, China, and Japan as leading contributors and the United
  States as a collaboration hub, and a keyword shift toward treatment,
  life stage, and diagnosis.
  [PMID 43063100](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43063100/) — *why it matters:* a map of papers. It is not
  a clinical result.

## Pharmacology and supportive care

- **Yoga versus structured exercise** — described by the authors as a
  prospective randomized clinical trial at two tertiary centres in São
  Paulo. PubMed's publication type is journal article. 52 women aged
  18–45 with endometriosis confirmed by imaging or by laparoscopy with
  histology were randomized 1:1 to eight weekly online sessions. After
  losses, the analysed groups were yoga n=37 and exercise n=15. Both
  groups improved in dysmenorrhea, deep dyspareunia, acyclic pelvic
  pain, and dyschezia, and on most SF-36 and EHP-30 domains. Group-by-time
  interactions favored exercise for the EHP-30 work domain and yoga for
  "relationship with child/children" and "feelings about treatment."
  [PMID 43098195](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43098195/) — *why it matters:* both groups changed, the
  analysed groups are unequal, and there is no comparison with usual
  care alone. It is not an exercise prescription.
- **Dienogest versus a combined pill** — described by the authors as a
  randomized parallel-group clinical trial in patients with endometrioma.
  PubMed's publication type is journal article. Fifty patients were
  enrolled in each group. One group received Desoceptive LD and the
  other dienogest, with mapping ultrasound and symptom history at
  baseline, 6 months, and 12 months. Endometrioma size decreased more
  with dienogest (P value = 0.00, as printed). Dysmenorrhea (P=0.003)
  and dyschezia (P value = 0.00, as printed) favored dienogest. Chronic
  pelvic pain was P=0.051 and dyspareunia P=0.87. The abstract does not
  give the size of those differences. The authors say future studies are
  recommended.
  [PMID 43080891](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43080891/) — *why it matters:* one trial whose abstract
  omits the effect size. It is not a regimen to copy, and it does not
  change the linzagolix page. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **Adolescent adenomyosis and endometriosis medicines** — narrative
  review of English full texts, 2000–2026, including a restatement of
  international guidelines and the authors' experience-based adaptations.
  They say inhibiting ovulation and suppressing menstruation is a
  rational first pharmacological step, that approved therapies control
  symptoms and do not cure the disease, that low-dose combined oral
  contraceptives and progestogen monotherapy are generally used for
  predominantly nociceptive pain, and that GnRH analogues may suit mixed
  nociceptive and nociplastic pain, with add-back when agonists or
  high-dose antagonists are used for amenorrhea.
  [PMID 43077591](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43077591/) — *why it matters:* a review of existing
  practice. It is not itself a guideline, and it is not adopted as an
  OpenEndo protocol.
- **Family-centered education and quality of life** — randomized trial,
  one clinic in Mashhad. 64 women with clinically and ultrasonographically
  confirmed endometriosis: 32 received a 12-week family-centered
  empowerment program and 32 received routine care. Adjusted EHP-30
  differences (intervention minus control) were pain −8.87 (95% CI
  −13.02 to −4.72), control and powerlessness −5.36 (95% CI −7.41 to
  −3.31), emotional well-being −6.71 (95% CI −9.38 to −4.04), social
  support −4.79 (95% CI −6.93 to −2.65), and self-image −2.70 (95% CI
  −3.93 to −1.47). All five remained significant after a Bonferroni
  threshold of 0.01. Registered IRCT20220720055505N1.
  [PMID 43079493](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43079493/) — *why it matters:* one clinic, 12 weeks,
  questionnaire scores. The authors ask for larger multicenter follow-up
  before durability is known. It is not a service model. Indexed in the
  edat supplement.
- **A serious-game trial that has not reported results** — protocol for
  a Swedish multicenter randomized trial. 250 participants are planned
  at four national centres for advanced endometriosis surgery, comparing
  the EndoExpedition game with written information. The primary outcome
  is patient satisfaction immediately afterwards (Quality from the
  Patient's Perspective). Ethics approval Dnr 2024-07580-01. Registered
  NCT07758946. No outcome data are in this record.
  [PMID 43072846](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43072846/) — *why it matters:* a plan. It is not evidence
  that a game improves knowledge or decisions.
- **Short-course dienogest during ovarian stimulation, one patient** —
  a 26-year-old nulligravid woman with stage IV disease after a small-bowel
  obstruction. Dienogest 2 mg daily was continued, without another
  ovulation-blocking drug, during stimulation. Baseline anti-Müllerian
  hormone was 7.5 pmol/L (1.05 ng/mL) and antral follicle count was 3.
  On stimulation day 13, estradiol was 3280 pmol/L (893.4 pg/mL) and
  three follicles were over 17 mm. Eleven mature oocytes were
  cryopreserved. Surgery followed three weeks later. The authors say one
  case cannot establish safety, efficacy, or duration.
  [PMID 43081508](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43081508/) — *why it matters:* a single stimulation. Not
  a fertility-preservation protocol. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **Mind-body practices and supplements for chronic pelvic pain** —
  systematic review, PROSPERO CRD42023424978, search through April 2023.
  28 randomized trials, 1,414 women, of gynecologic chronic pelvic pain
  rather than endometriosis alone. Designs were too different for a
  meta-analysis. The authors say multimodal mind-body practices had the
  most consistent benefit, without one shared method, and that
  nutritional supplements showed promising adjunctive potential in
  endometriosis-associated pelvic pain. The abstract names no supplement
  and no effect size. No study evaluated cost.
  [PMID 43067081](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43067081/) — *why it matters:* a review that does not
  identify a supplement or a dose. Nothing here is adopted as a
  supplement or as a mind-body protocol. Indexed in the edat supplement.

## Notable clinical signals

- **No new guideline or Cochrane update.** The adolescent review
  (PMID 43077591) restates hormonal practice and is not itself a
  guideline. There is no new ESHRE, NICE final guidance, or regulatory
  decision in the included set. The BMJ editorial (PMID 43078525) was
  only partly readable. Its opening says draft NICE guidance recommends
  Endotest and that the authors are concerned the evidence may not
  justify that recommendation. That does not change the
  saliva-diagnostics page.
- **Nothing here changes an OpenEndo repurposing verdict, the Yselty
  page, the saliva-diagnostics page, or the lesion-cell ferroptosis
  sentence.** The multiancestry study (PMID 43064779; 58 regions, 314
  genes) does not name a drug to advance. GLP-1 receptor agonists
  (PMID 43097232) are explicitly not shown to work in endometriosis.
  RRx-001 (PMID 43084987) is a mouse experiment and is not an M3
  candidate. Lopinavir and GSK-429286A (PMID 43075688) are computational
  hits, not verdict changes. The rat formula that activated ferroptosis
  (PMID 43062770) is consistent with the existing direction sentence and
  is not a medicine. The ovarian-cancer transcriptomic paper
  (PMID 43087691) says it does not establish ferroptosis activity. No
  sulfasalazine, sirolimus, or MRGPRX2 paper is in the included set.
  Dienogest papers do not move the linzagolix page.
- **Non-invasive tests in this window are not standards.** The omics
  review (PMID 43095119) says no current assay should replace
  established pathways, and it places salivary microRNA at discovery
  stage. Electroviscerography (PMID 43077756; specificity 21.05% against
  ultrasound, negative predictive value 47.06%) is not a rule-out test.
  The endometrioma nomogram (PMID 43079448) applies only after
  endometriosis is already confirmed on pathology. KL/TLE1
  (PMID 43072262) and the one-hospital model (PMID 43065352; validation
  area under the curve 0.878) are not clinical tests. Laparoscopy
  remains the statement on the public explainer for definitive
  diagnosis.
- **Delay figures in this window are not population estimates.** The
  French cohort (PMID 43078066) estimated a median 8.9 years (IQR
  6.9–9.8) from a two-event healthcare proxy to the first hospital
  diagnosis in 9,088 women aged 18–25. That is not adopted. The BMJ
  editorial's "averaging nine years" sentence is a description of
  public debate in the unread-full-text opening, and it is not adopted.
  The WHO 4–12 year range stands.
- **Two activity trials and one education trial are not protocols.**
  Yoga and structured exercise both improved pain in an unbalanced
  analysis (PMID 43098195; 37 versus 15). Dienogest separated from a
  combined pill on some symptoms (PMID 43080891; 50 per group) without
  an effect size in the abstract. Family-centered education moved five
  EHP-30 domains at one clinic (PMID 43079493; 32 versus 32). None is
  adopted as a program.
- **Surgery papers do not choose an operation.** Bowel involvement after
  fertility-preserving surgery had pregnancy and live-birth intervals
  that include 1 (PMID 43090964; 202 women). Quality-of-life scores
  improved by six months in 50 women who also took a hormone
  (PMID 43090905), without a non-surgical comparison and without a
  detected difference among the hormones. Six trocar-site lesions
  (PMID 43075461) and one inguinal lesion (PMID 43098182) are cases.
- **The supplement review does not name a supplement.** PMID 43067081
  (28 trials, 1,414 women with chronic pelvic pain) says nutritional
  supplements looked promising for endometriosis-associated pain and
  gives no product and no effect size. The boron-curcumin-piperine
  experiment (PMID 43093352) is in vitro, four donors per group. Neither
  is adopted.
- **Donor insemination and allergy are associations.** Endometriosis was
  associated with a lower cumulative live birth after donor-sperm
  insemination (PMID 43072032; adjusted OR 0.5, 95% CI 0.2–0.9) in women
  the authors describe as without known infertility. Allergic disease diagnosed by age 10 was associated with later
  endometriosis in Ontario (PMID 43098290; adjusted HR 1.51, 95% CI
  1.29–1.77). The abstract says the association was particularly seen
  with asthma, and that the hazard was smaller when allergic disease was
  diagnosed later.
  Neither paper is a reason to change a pathway.
