## Diagnostics and imaging

- **Suggestive deep and ovarian disease on routine postmenopausal ultrasound** —
  prospective series at two regional clinics (NCT07503938), 24 months.
  Consecutive postmenopausal women attending for a routine examination had
  transvaginal ultrasound by one operator using International Deep
  Endometriosis Analysis (IDEA) criteria. 492 participants (mean age
  60.2 ± 6.1 years; median 8.0 years since menopause). Findings suggestive
  of endometriosis were recorded in 40/492 (8.1%; 95% CI 5.9%–10.9%),
  including posterior-compartment hypoechoic nodules in 36 (7.3%).
  Intestinal nodules were 21 (4.3%), often plaque-like, thinner than 7 mm,
  with regular margins, limited Doppler signal, and uncommon submucosal
  involvement. Posterior nodules were 13.9% within 5 years of menopause
  and 4.3% after more than 10 years (p for trend = 0.001). In multivariable
  analysis, ≤5 years since menopause had an adjusted odds ratio of 3.25
  (95% CI 1.35–7.83), a history of dysmenorrhea 2.20 (95% CI 1.05–4.60),
  and chronic pelvic pain 2.36 (95% CI 1.11–5.00). Current pelvic pain,
  dyschezia, previous infertility, and systemic hormone replacement were
  not independently associated. Six cysts suggestive of endometrioma were
  recorded (1.2%; 95% CI 0.45%–2.64%); five of the six had atypical
  features (multilocularity, heterogeneous echoes, papillary excrescences,
  or wall thickening).
  [PMID 43001842](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43001842/) — *why it matters:* these are ultrasound appearances, not a
  histology-confirmed prevalence, and not a screening program. The
  atypical cysts are a reason not to assume a postmenopausal mass is
  benign. This does not change the need for histology when a mass is
  suspicious.
- **Standardized IDEA image documentation, before and after** —
  single-centre retrospective before-and-after study at Peking University
  Shenzhen Hospital. 783 patients had a dedicated deep-disease ultrasound
  and then laparoscopy within 6 months with surgical and pathological
  confirmation (January 2023–September 2025). The protocol, started
  November 2024, required 19 standard sections. 267 examinations were
  before the protocol and 516 after. Documentation rose for 15 of 19
  sections after Bonferroni correction (P<0.0026), with absolute gains of
  47.9%–63.3% for sections that had been under-recorded, including the
  uterovesical pouch, ureters, rectovaginal septum, and lower rectum.
  Median examination time was unchanged (719 vs 723 seconds; P=0.529).
  Specificity for pouch-of-Douglas obliteration rose from 80.0% to 93.9%
  (P<0.001) and for rectosigmoid involvement from 79.9% to 90.0%
  (P<0.001). Sensitivity of the uterine sliding sign rose from 74.0% to
  95.1% (P<0.001). Adenomyosis sensitivity also rose (82.5% to 94.9%;
  P=0.003); the authors treat adenomyosis and ovarian endometrioma as
  related findings, not as deep-disease sites. In 14 patients with
  ureteral deep disease, median ureteral diameter near the bladder was
  3.13 mm (IQR 2.70–3.30) on the involved side and 2.15 mm (IQR 1.90–2.50)
  on the other side (P<0.001).
  [PMID 43009933](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43009933/) — *why it matters:* a documentation checklist at one hospital, not a
  new test and not a multicentre accuracy standard. Epub 2026-08-12;
  indexed in the edat supplement.
- **CRP–triglyceride–glucose index versus CRP** — case-control study.
  153 women with surgically confirmed endometriosis and 112 with no
  visible endometriosis at laparoscopy. Triglycerides, total cholesterol,
  erythrocyte sedimentation rate, C-reactive protein (CRP), and the
  combined index (CTI) were higher in the endometriosis group (all
  P<0.001). Fasting glucose did not differ. CTI discrimination had an
  area under the curve of 0.900 (95% CI 0.858–0.942). An exploratory
  cutoff of 9.686 had sensitivity 92.16% and specificity 83.93%. After
  adjustment for age, body mass index, and blood pressure, the adjusted
  odds ratio was 2.598 per 0.5-unit increase (95% CI 2.079–3.245). CTI
  did not differ from CRP alone (AUC 0.898; P=0.827). Adding
  triglycerides to a CRP model improved fit (P=0.007); adding glucose
  did not (P=0.095). The difference in areas across the nested models
  was not significant (P=0.719).
  [PMID 43017562](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43017562/) — *why it matters:* a selected surgical case-control, not a blood test
  to use. The index did not beat CRP. The authors call it complementary
  to clinical assessment and imaging.
- **Giant endometrioma on 18F-FDG PET/CT** — one case. A 27-year-old woman
  had a giant pelvic cystic mass read as possible malignancy, with weight
  loss and elevated tumor markers. PET/CT showed a cystic lesion filling
  the abdominopelvic cavity, mild internal septations, and mild FDG
  uptake. Histopathology was bilateral ovarian endometriomas.
  [PMID 43019234](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43019234/) — *why it matters:* one confirmed benign mass. Mild FDG uptake is not a
  rule for telling an endometrioma from cancer.

## Surgery

- **Conservative versus segmental surgery for bowel endometriosis** —
  retrospective single-centre series, November 2020–October 2024.
  91 consecutive patients had surgery for colorectal deep endometriosis:
  40 (44%) conservative (shaving or full-thickness disc resection) and
  51 (56%) segmental resection. Clavien–Dindo grade III complications
  occurred in 19/91 (20.9%): 6/40 (15%) after conservative surgery and
  13/51 (25.5%) after segmental resection (p=0.22). Bowel leakage was
  4/40 (10%) and 6/51 (11.8%) (p=0.405). One rectovaginal fistula followed
  segmental resection (1/51, 1.9%). No grade IV complication was recorded.
  Segmental resection took longer (295 minutes, 95% CI 267–322, versus
  201 minutes, 95% CI 184–217; p<0.0001) and the hospital stay was longer
  (mean 11.57 ± 10.96 days versus 5.78 ± 4.2 days; p<0.001). Visual
  analogue pain scores, including dysmenorrhea, non-cyclic pelvic pain,
  dyspareunia, and dyschezia, fell in both groups at 6 months (p<0.001).
  The abstract does not give the point change.
  [PMID 43018427](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43018427/) — *why it matters:* complication rates were not separated, and pain
  fell after both operations. This is not a trial of which bowel
  technique to choose. The groups were not randomized.
- **Robot-assisted versus conventional laparoscopy for colorectal disease** —
  systematic review and meta-analysis of comparative observational
  cohorts, PRISMA 2020, searched January 2020–August 2026. Studies of
  general, superficial, ovarian, or deep disease without a separable
  colorectal group were excluded. Eight reports met the criteria. Where
  operative time was reported, robot-assisted surgery was generally
  longer; the size of that difference varied. Estimated blood loss,
  overall postoperative complications, conversion to open surgery, and
  most other perioperative outcomes were broadly comparable. Length of
  stay was inconsistent. Some reports suggested differences in voiding
  dysfunction, reoperation, recurrence, or opioid use. The authors say
  heterogeneity was considerable, that several reports may overlap from
  the same centre, and that overlap was handled at outcome level. No
  pooled estimate is given in the abstract.
  [PMID 42997287](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42997287/) — *why it matters:* observational cohorts, not a reason to prefer a
  robot. The authors say causal conclusions cannot be drawn. Issue date
  2026 Oct; indexed in the edat supplement.
- **Clear-cell carcinoma in a cesarean-scar endometriotic nodule** — one
  case. A 48-year-old woman, cesarean delivery 20 years earlier, had a
  growing abdominal-wall mass and constitutional symptoms. MRI showed a
  solid infiltrative lesion and regional lymphadenopathy. Core biopsy
  suggested metastatic serous carcinoma. After three cycles of
  carboplatin and paclitaxel she had wide excision, hysterectomy,
  bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy, pelvic lymphadenectomy, and
  omentectomy. Final histology was clear-cell carcinoma arising in
  abdominal-wall endometriosis, with one involved inguinal node and no
  gynecologic primary. Adjuvant chemotherapy and maintenance
  bevacizumab followed. Imaging showed no residual disease, and she was
  disease-free 2 years after surgery.
  [PMID 43006091](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43006091/) — *why it matters:* one malignancy in a scar nodule. It is not a
  surveillance rule for every cesarean scar, and the chemotherapy
  sequence is what was done for this patient. Indexed in the edat
  supplement.
- **Vulvar endometriosis as a periurethral mass** — one case. A
  44-year-old woman had unilateral swelling of the upper labium majus
  and deviation of the urethral meatus, starting 5 years after a
  spontaneous vaginal delivery. The lesion was excised. Histology was
  vulvar endometriosis.
  [PMID 43006066](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43006066/) — *why it matters:* one extrapelvic nodule confirmed after excision.
  It is not a diagnostic pathway. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **Catamenial hemopneumothorax from diaphragmatic endometriosis** — one
  case. A 44-year-old woman had recurrent right hemopneumothorax within
  72 hours of menstruation. Chest radiography and computed tomography
  showed effusion and pneumothorax and did not identify a cause.
  Video-assisted thoracoscopy found about 500 ml of old blood, dark-red
  nodules on the right hemidiaphragm, and a 1 cm diaphragmatic defect.
  The affected diaphragm was stapled and repaired, with pleurodesis.
  Histology confirmed diaphragmatic endometriosis. No recurrence was
  recorded at 3 months. The authors' learning point pairs that operation
  with postoperative hormonal suppression. The case description does not
  say hormonal suppression was given.
  [PMID 43012040](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43012040/) — *why it matters:* one operation with short follow-up. Normal chest
  imaging did not exclude diaphragmatic disease in this patient. Hormonal
  suppression is not adopted from the learning point. Indexed in the
  edat supplement.

## Mechanisms and biology

- **REST and progesterone responsiveness** — human immunohistochemistry
  and a uterine-specific Rest knockout mouse. Nuclear REST was reduced
  in eutopic endometrium and ectopic lesions compared with control
  endometrium. The abstract does not give the number of patients. Rest
  knockout females had progressive subfertility and a blunted response
  to progesterone, with progesterone receptor expression preserved.
  Lesions grown from Rest-deficient donor tissue were larger and less
  responsive to progesterone than lesions from control tissue. Mechanical
  sensitivity was modestly increased; vaginal hyperalgesia was
  unaffected.
  [PMID 43013554](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43013554/) — *why it matters:* a mouse and tissue finding. REST is not a drug, and
  the paper does not report a human treatment.
- **HDAC3 with the progesterone receptor** — mouse uterus, with
  co-immunoprecipitation, chromatin profiling, and immunostaining that
  includes infertile women with endometriosis. HDAC3 and the
  progesterone receptor share target genes and co-occupy regulatory
  regions of progesterone-responsive genes. Their expression correlated
  positively in stromal cells in controls and in infertile women with
  endometriosis; the epithelial correlation was kept in a subset of the
  infertile cases. The paper's background states that 30%–50% of women
  with endometriosis are affected by infertility. That range is the
  authors' framing. It is not adopted here as a new figure.
  [PMID 42996627](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42996627/) — *why it matters:* a proposed coregulator of progesterone signalling.
  It is not a trial of an HDAC inhibitor. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **TREM2-positive macrophages and CD8 T cells** — single-cell RNA
  sequencing of 11 clinical samples, a TREM2 knockout mouse, co-culture,
  and a tool compound. TREM2-positive macrophages were enriched in
  endometriosis. CD8-positive T cells and natural killer cells in
  lesions showed exhaustion-associated features and reduced
  cytotoxicity. Communication analysis pointed at SPP1–CD44 and at
  BAG3-associated autophagy. TREM2 deficiency increased cytotoxicity and
  reduced ectopic lesion growth. YM-1, used to disrupt the BAG3–HSP70
  interaction, impaired CD8 T-cell function and promoted lesion growth.
  BAG3 silencing reduced autophagy-related markers and effector function
  in CD8 T cells.
  [PMID 43016249](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43016249/) — *why it matters:* a mouse and co-culture result. YM-1 made lesions
  larger in this experiment. It is not a treatment.
- **Mast-cell CCL2 and neuronal CCR4** — re-analysis of public
  single-cell data (GSE179640), patient tissue, stromal–mast-cell–neuron
  co-culture, and a rat model. CCL2 was higher in endometriosis and
  correlated with pain, mast-cell infiltration, and
  protein-gene-product-9.5-positive nerve fibres. The abstract does not
  give the number of patients. Knockdown of CCL2 in mast cells inhibited
  degranulation and migration and, in the co-culture, promoted neurite
  outgrowth, nerve-growth-factor expression, and calcium influx.
  Inhibiting CCR4 in the rat relieved hyperalgesia and reduced nerve
  fibres in lesions.
  [PMID 42992652](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42992652/) — *why it matters:* a mast-cell chemokine result in tissue and in a
  rat. It is not an MRGPRX2 experiment, and the MRGPRX2 knowledge page
  is unchanged. It is not a drug.
- **Lemon-derived vesicles and macrophage ferroptosis markers** —
  single-cell transcriptomes of eutopic endometrium from women with
  endometriosis and from controls, plus RAW264.7 macrophages and an
  experimental endometriosis model. The abstract does not give the
  number of women or animals. An oxidative-stress macrophage state was
  identified, with ACSL1 as a candidate node. Lemon-derived
  extracellular vesicles increased GPX4 mRNA and decreased TFRC and
  ACSL1 in the macrophage line. Under RSL3, pretreatment attenuated the
  rise in ACSL4. In the experimental model, vesicle treatment reduced
  ectopic lesion burden and collagen, with increased GPX4 staining and
  decreased ACSL1 and ACSL4 staining.
  [PMID 43020163](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43020163/) — *why it matters:* vesicles in a cell line and an experimental model,
  acting on macrophage markers. This is not a food, a supplement, or a
  lesion-cell ferroptosis experiment. The lesion-cell ferroptosis
  sentence on the public explainer is unchanged. The sulfasalazine
  watchlist is unchanged.
- **Ferroptosis-associated macrophages and an ACSL1–PGE2–iron proposal** —
  public single-cell data from eutopic endometrium, bulk RNA sequencing,
  and in-vitro macrophage and stromal-cell assays. The abstract does not
  give a patient count. Ferroptosis-associated macrophages were enriched
  in the endometriosis samples. Ferrostatin-1 reduced erastin-induced
  lipid peroxidation and injury in macrophages. ACSL1 knockdown reduced
  ferroptosis-related changes and PGE2 secretion. In endometriotic
  stromal cells, PGE2 lowered FTH1, raised SLC40A1, and raised
  extracellular Fe³⁺; EP2 blockade partly reversed those changes. Iron
  chelation reduced iron-loading injury in macrophages. The authors
  propose a macrophage–stromal axis with possible intervention points at
  ACSL1, PGE2/EP2, and iron handling.
  [PMID 43007482](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43007482/) — *why it matters:* a proposed axis in cultured cells. Ferrostatin-1
  and an iron chelator here are tool treatments of macrophages, not
  medicines. This does not move the lesion-cell ferroptosis direction
  or the sulfasalazine watchlist.
- **Long-chain acyl-CoA synthetases across several reproductive diseases** —
  narrative review of ACSL1, ACSL3, ACSL4, ACSL5, and ACSL6 in cervical,
  ovarian, and endometrial cancers, polycystic ovary syndrome,
  endometriosis, and preeclampsia. The authors say the most consistent
  disease-relevant role is ACSL4 in ferroptosis regulation, and that the
  other isoforms are more context-dependent. They separate
  disease-specific experiments from bioinformatic associations and from
  extrapolation.
  [PMID 43017016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43017016/) — *why it matters:* a cross-disease review, not a new lesion
  measurement. It does not change the lesion-cell ferroptosis sentence.
  Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **m6A methylation screen** — human m6A microarray of mRNA and long
  non-coding RNA, with follow-up by public data, quantitative PCR,
  immunohistochemistry, and immunofluorescence. The abstract does not
  give the number of samples. The authors report 1,631 hyper-upregulated,
  219 hypo-upregulated, and 6,483 hypo-downregulated genes that were both
  differentially methylated and differentially expressed, eight candidate
  hub genes (RHOA, KCNA2, BDNF, CACNA1C, FGFR1, IL18, KCNIP2, RPL17),
  six hypermethylated and 14 hypomethylated long non-coding RNAs, and
  two candidate m6A regulators, WTAP and RBM15.
  [PMID 43017545](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43017545/) — *why it matters:* an exploratory screen. The authors say the profiles
  are correlations, not causal relationships. Not a test and not a drug
  target to use.
- **Nanomaterials review** — narrative review of targeted delivery,
  immunomodulation, and physical ablation (photothermal gold particles
  and magnetic hyperthermia) for endometriosis. The authors say existing
  hormonal and surgical treatment is limited by recurrence and side
  effects, and that clinical validation of these materials is still
  required.
  [PMID 43005712](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43005712/) — *why it matters:* a preclinical concept review. None of these
  materials is a treatment here. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **Zinc and copper as a computational repurposing idea** — comparison of
  gene expression between unaffected endometrium and endometriosis-affected
  tissue, then a search for existing compounds that might hit the shared
  genes. The authors say endometriosis-associated genes and
  infertility-associated genes overlap, and they name zinc and copper.
  The abstract does not report a new animal or human experiment.
  [PMID 42993059](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42993059/) — *why it matters:* a computer shortlist. The title's "promising
  therapy" is the authors' conclusion and is not adopted. Zinc and copper
  are not OpenEndo repurposing candidates. This is not a supplement
  recommendation. The Yselty page is unchanged.

## Epidemiology and burden

- **Who records the diagnosis at laparoscopy** — Freedom of Information
  requests to 143 NHS organisations. 52 returned analysable data on
  diagnostic laparoscopies in women aged 16–35, 2013–2023. The authors'
  background states that endometriosis affects 10% of women of
  reproductive age and that the average diagnostic delay is 7 years. The
  7-year figure is not adopted. This repo keeps the WHO fact-sheet range
  of 4–12 years. The returned data: 1,419,366 admissions for abdominal
  pain in general surgery and gynecology, and 4,356 endometriosis
  diagnoses per year in the analysed returns. General surgeons diagnosed
  endometriosis in 255/8,305 laparoscopies (3.1%; 95% CI 2.7%–3.5%).
  Gynecologists diagnosed it in 4,825/21,456 (22.5%; 95% CI
  21.9%–23.1%). Delayed diagnosis after an inconclusive procedure was
  8.2% in general surgery and 15.5% in gynecology. Complaint rates, from
  four trusts, did not differ (p=0.49).
  [PMID 43016060](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43016060/) — *why it matters:* a specialty comparison of coded diagnoses, not a
  population prevalence and not a training standard. Case mix and coding
  can contribute. The authors say the gap still supports better
  recognition. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **Ten years of histology at one South African laboratory** —
  retrospective review of archival cases at Dr George Mukhari Academic
  Laboratory, 1 January 2012–31 December 2021. Of 93,804 female
  specimens, 156 were histologically confirmed endometriosis (0.166% of
  specimens). Mean age 36.97 years. 60.3% showed all three classic
  histologic features. The ovary was the most frequent site (36.5%).
  More than one site was involved in 9.6%, mostly the adnexa.
  Endometriotic cysts were present in 28.8%. Associated lesions were
  recorded in 24.4%. Cervical endometriosis was associated with age 50
  or older (p=0.008), in a small number of cases. The paper's background
  cites about 190 million women globally.
  [PMID 43011181](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43011181/) — *why it matters:* a share of laboratory specimens, not a population
  prevalence. The 0.166% figure is not how common the disease is.
  Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **Neonicotinoids in follicular fluid** — 174 infertile women in
  assisted reproduction, 63 with endometriosis and 111 controls. Sixteen
  neonicotinoids and metabolites were measured. N-desmethyl-acetamiprid
  was the most often detected. Each twofold increase was associated with
  higher odds of prevalent endometriosis (adjusted OR 1.79; 95% CI
  1.38–2.32) and was prioritized in mixture models. Several compounds
  were also associated with fewer mature oocytes, fewer two-pronuclear
  zygotes, and lower pregnancy odds, with stronger patterns in the
  endometriosis group. Untargeted metabolomics in 40 women found 324
  features differing by endometriosis at nominal P<0.05, of which 54
  remained after false-discovery correction. 47 features had nominal
  associations with both N-desmethyl-acetamiprid and endometriosis.
  [PMID 42994776](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42994776/) — *why it matters:* a cross-sectional exposure association in women
  already attending for assisted reproduction. It is not evidence that
  avoiding an insecticide treats endometriosis, and it is not a cause
  established by this design.
- **Circulating fatty acids, Mendelian randomization** — bidirectional
  two-sample Mendelian randomization of fatty-acid traits and female
  reproductive diagnoses, with multivariable adjustment for body mass
  index and lipids. After outlier removal and false-discovery
  correction, genetically predicted monounsaturated fatty acids were
  associated with endometriosis (inverse-variance weighted OR 1.15;
  95% CI 1.02–1.29; P=0.0206; FDR=0.0412). That estimate strengthened
  after removal of rs12212146. DHA was associated with infertility
  (OR 1.16; 95% CI 1.04–1.29) only after removal of rs12639836. Neither
  association remained after multivariable adjustment for body mass
  index, triglycerides, HDL cholesterol, or LDL cholesterol. No reverse
  association remained after false-discovery correction.
  [PMID 43008605](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43008605/) — *why it matters:* a genetic association that did not hold after lipid
  and body-mass adjustment. It is not diet advice. Indexed in the edat
  supplement; pubdate 2026 Nov 3, the day before this pdat window.
- **Trace elements and oxidative-stress markers** — 60 women
  (endometriosis and age-matched controls; the abstract does not split
  the 60). Arsenic, lead, mercury, copper, and iron were higher, and
  zinc and selenium were lower, in the endometriosis group. MDA, SOD,
  and 8-OHdG were higher and glutathione was lower (all p<0.001).
  Docking scores were Cu²⁺ with SOD −6.0 kcal/mol, Zn²⁺ with GPX1
  −6.3 kcal/mol, and MDA with SOD −4.72 kcal/mol.
  [PMID 43019809](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43019809/) — *why it matters:* a small case-control of blood levels plus docking.
  It is not a chelation protocol and not a zinc or selenium supplement.
  Pubdate 2026 Nov 5; indexed in the edat supplement.
- **Inflammatory markers before and after the COVID-19 pandemic** —
  paired samples from the Tehran Lipid and Glucose Study. Of 2,558 women,
  465 had an endometriosis diagnosis, and 13 had biobanked blood from
  both 2016–2018 and 2021–2023. Individual infection and vaccination were
  not recorded. TAC1, TACR1, VEGF, and CXCL5 expression did not change
  (confidence intervals included 1). Neutrophil-elastase expression after
  serum stimulation rose (fold change 1.5; 95% CI 1.01–2.25; P=0.048).
  PAD4, MMP9, MPO, and interleukin-6 did not differ (interleukin-6 median
  paired difference +0.58 pg/mL; 95% CI −0.89 to +2.34; P=0.750). The
  authors say the study was underpowered for small differences and that
  the non-significant results are inconclusive.
  [PMID 43001088](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43001088/) — *why it matters:* 13 pairs and one marker at the edge of the
  interval. It is not a pandemic effect and not a marker to use. Indexed
  in the edat supplement.
- **Endometriosis and chronic endometritis in an infertility clinic** —
  single-centre retrospective cohort, October 2019–December 2024.
  250 women with laparoscopically and histologically confirmed
  endometriosis and 750 with tubal-factor infertility and endometriosis
  excluded at laparoscopy. Chronic endometritis was diagnosed by CD38/CD138
  immunohistochemistry. Detection was higher in the endometriosis cohort
  (P<0.001); the abstract does not give that overall percentage. Within
  endometriosis, prevalence was 45.1% in stage III–IV disease and 24.0%
  in stage I–II (P=0.001). At one year the endometriosis cohort had a
  lower clinical pregnancy rate (70.0% vs 80.0%), a higher rate of
  persistent infertility (30.0% vs 20.0%), and a higher early pregnancy
  loss rate (16.0% vs 9.2%) than the tubal-factor cohort (all P<0.05).
  Within endometriosis, concurrent endometritis was associated with
  pregnancy 56.0% versus 76.0%, persistent infertility 44.0% versus
  24.0%, and early loss 31.0% versus 11.3% (all P=0.002). The authors say
  the reproductive comparisons were exploratory and unadjusted, and that
  the dataset is not enough for a universal diagnostic or treatment
  guideline.
  [PMID 43012803](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43012803/) — *why it matters:* a cross-sectional association at one clinic. Chronic
  endometritis testing is not adopted from this paper. Indexed in the
  edat supplement.
- **Coagulation measures and IVF** — retrospective cohort, 2022–2024.
  108 infertile women with endometriosis and 108 age-matched tubal-factor
  controls, first cycle and first transfer only. A coagulation
  abnormality was defined as fibrinogen >4.0 g/L, D-dimer >0.5 mg/L, or
  antithrombin III <75%. That definition was met by 52/108 (48.15%)
  versus 8/108 (7.41%) (P<0.001). PT, APTT, and TT were shorter, and
  fibrinogen and D-dimer higher, as continuous variables; those three
  times were not used in the abnormality definition. Among the
  endometriosis group, live birth across 0, 1, 2, and 3 abnormal
  parameters was 44.6%, 29.6%, 16.7%, and 0.0% (P for trend = 0.002).
  In fully adjusted models, elevated D-dimer had OR 2.864 for live-birth
  failure (95% CI 1.427–5.748) and lower antithrombin III had OR 0.512
  (95% CI 0.276–0.949). A higher rate of high-quality embryos was
  protective (OR 0.326; 95% CI 0.154–0.689). Fibrinogen was not
  significant (P=0.227). The D-dimer association remained after further
  adjustment for embryo quality but was attenuated. The authors say the
  findings are exploratory and that routine D-dimer and antithrombin III
  testing cannot currently be recommended.
  [PMID 42996662](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42996662/) — *why it matters:* an association in one IVF cohort. It is not a
  clotting work-up to add, and the authors say so. Indexed in the edat
  supplement.
- **Vaginal bacteria and a questionnaire on endocrine-disrupting exposures** —
  exploratory case-control study at Amiens University Hospital, January
  2024–June 2025. 25 women with surgically confirmed stage III–IV
  endometriosis and 25 controls had vaginal samples (culture, Nugent
  score, and quantitative PCR for 11 species). A wider questionnaire
  cohort was 261 women (137 with endometriosis, 124 controls), including
  those 50. Nugent intermediate flora was 45.5% versus 30.0%, and
  bacterial-vaginosis profiles were 13.6% versus 15.0% (p>0.05). Total
  16S biomass did not differ (p=0.17). *Atopobium vaginae* (p=0.001) and
  *Lactobacillus crispatus* (p=0.018) were lower, and *Lactobacillus
  gasseri* was higher (p=0.008). Total Lactobacillus did not differ
  (p=0.26). Insecticide-diffuser use was reported more often by women
  with endometriosis (29% vs 16%; p=0.02).
  [PMID 42996596](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42996596/) — *why it matters:* 25 versus 25, exploratory. It is not a vaginal test
  and not evidence that a household product causes endometriosis.
  Indexed in the edat supplement.

## Pharmacology and supportive care

- **TENS trial protocol, no result** — planned randomized crossover trial
  at 27 sites in France (NCT07317037). 208 women with chronic pelvic pain
  from endometriosis are to use actiTENS or low-dose TENS for at least
  60 minutes twice daily, in two 3-month periods, then an open-label
  phase. The primary outcome is the change in average pain over the last
  28 days of each 3-month period. Secondary outcomes include quality of
  life, EHP-30, analgesic use, and device use. No participant result is
  reported.
  [PMID 43013290](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43013290/) — *why it matters:* a protocol. It is not evidence that TENS relieves
  endometriosis pain.
- **Digital CBT, six women** — mixed-methods study of the DAHLIA
  program (NCT05066087): six self-guided modules plus therapist
  contact. Six women with endometriosis were interviewed. They valued
  phone or video contact and described feeling acknowledged. Time demand
  was a difficulty. Patient-reported measures, scored with the Reliable
  Change Index, suggested less pain and mixed results for psychological
  symptoms and pain acceptance. The abstract does not give the score
  changes.
  [PMID 42995486](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42995486/) — *why it matters:* six people and no control arm in this report. It is
  not a treatment to offer on the strength of this paper. Indexed in the
  edat supplement.
- **Microbial and plant-derived interventions in PMOS and endometriosis** —
  narrative review. The authors use PMOS for polycystic ovary syndrome.
  They describe oxidative stress and inflammation as shared processes,
  then separate the evidence. Selected probiotic, synbiotic, and
  phytochemical trials in polycystic ovary syndrome are described as
  reporting metabolic, endocrine, and oxidative changes. For
  endometriosis, they say human intervention findings are concentrated
  in pain outcomes, and that evidence about changing the lesion itself
  is still mostly experimental. Formulation, compartment, phenotype, and
  concomitant treatment affect interpretation. No endometriosis-specific
  pooled effect is reported.
  [PMID 43012368](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43012368/) — *why it matters:* a review that does not replace standard treatment
  with a probiotic or a plant product. That limit is recorded and is not
  adopted as advice. Indexed in the edat supplement.
- **Diet, the estrobolome, and a prevention framework** — narrative
  review. The authors' background states that endometriosis affects about
  5%–10% of reproductive-aged women. They organise diet through a
  proposed sequence of food-group epidemiology, gut microbiota, and
  estrogen-metabolizing bacteria, then place that sequence inside a
  wider prevention sketch. The sketch includes, as a conceptual proposal,
  targeted early hormonal intervention following a model from Vercellini
  and colleagues. They say six reviews and meta-analyses from 2024–2026
  have pooled dietary and nutraceutical trials for pain, and that this
  paper does not add another pooled estimate. They call the framework
  hypothesis-generating and not a set of established preventive measures.
  Endometriosis-specific cost-effectiveness of prevention and screening
  is described as scarce.
  [PMID 42996903](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42996903/) — *why it matters:* a research agenda. It is not a diet, a probiotic, or
  an early-hormone protocol. The early-hormone sentence is the authors'
  conceptual proposal and is not adopted. Indexed in the edat supplement.

## Notable clinical signals

- **No new guideline, Cochrane update, or regulatory decision** this week.
  Nothing here changes an OpenEndo repurposing verdict. Nothing here is
  adopted as a patient-facing treatment recommendation. Knowledge pages
  for Yselty and for saliva diagnostics are unchanged. The lesion-cell
  ferroptosis sentence on the public explainer is unchanged.
- **Postmenopausal ultrasound recorded suggestive findings in 40 of 492
  women (8.1%)** (PMID 43001842). That is not histology, and six
  endometrioma-like cysts included five with atypical features. It is not
  a screening program. A separate before-and-after study of IDEA image
  documentation (PMID 43009933; 783 patients, one centre) raised
  specificity for pouch-of-Douglas obliteration from 80.0% to 93.9% and
  for rectosigmoid involvement from 79.9% to 90.0%. That is a
  documentation practice at one hospital, not a new diagnostic standard.
  The CRP–triglyceride–glucose index (PMID 43017562) had AUC 0.900 and
  did not beat CRP (AUC 0.898). It is not a blood test.
- **Bowel surgery in 91 patients did not separate grade III complications
  by technique** (PMID 43018427; 19/91, p=0.22). Segmental resection took
  longer and the stay was longer. Pain fell in both groups. A review of
  eight observational comparisons (PMID 42997287) did not find a
  consistent perioperative advantage for robot-assisted colorectal
  surgery. Neither paper chooses an operation.
- **General surgeons recorded an endometriosis diagnosis at 255 of 8,305
  laparoscopies, and gynecologists at 4,825 of 21,456** (PMID 43016060).
  The paper's 7-year average delay is not adopted. The WHO fact-sheet
  range of 4–12 years stands. The South African series (PMID 43011181)
  is 156 of 93,804 specimens (0.166%). That is a laboratory share, not a
  population prevalence.
- **Two macrophage papers and one ACSL review do not move ferroptosis.**
  Lemon-derived vesicles raised GPX4 and lowered ACSL4 staining while
  lesion burden fell in an experimental model (PMID 43020163). A second
  paper proposes an ACSL1–PGE2–iron axis in macrophages and stromal cells
  (PMID 43007482). A cross-disease review says ACSL4 is the most
  consistent ferroptosis-related isoform (PMID 43017016). None of these
  is a lesion-cell induction experiment, a human trial, or a reason to
  change the sulfasalazine watchlist.
- **Zinc and copper (PMID 42993059) are a computational shortlist, not a
  therapy.** They are not OpenEndo candidates. The trace-element
  case-control (PMID 43019809; 60 women, split not stated) is blood
  levels and docking, not a supplement. The fatty-acid Mendelian
  randomization estimate (PMID 43008605; OR 1.15) did not survive
  adjustment for body mass and lipids.
- **TENS (PMID 43013290) is a protocol with no result** (NCT07317037,
  planned n=208). Digital CBT was described by six women (PMID 42995486).
  The probiotic and plant-product review (PMID 43012368) says
  endometriosis human data are concentrated in pain scores and that
  lesion biology is still mostly experimental. The diet review
  (PMID 42996903) calls itself hypothesis-generating. Its sentence on
  early hormonal intervention is a conceptual proposal and is not
  adopted.
- **Two fertility-clinic associations are not work-ups.** Chronic
  endometritis was more often detected in stage III–IV than stage I–II
  endometriosis (45.1% vs 24.0%; PMID 43012803); the authors say this is
  not a guideline. D-dimer and antithrombin III were associated with
  live-birth failure in one IVF cohort (PMID 42996662); the authors say
  routine testing cannot currently be recommended. That recommendation
  stands: neither test is added here.
- **One cesarean-scar clear-cell carcinoma (PMID 43006091) and one
  diaphragmatic hemopneumothorax (PMID 43012040) are single cases.** The
  thoracic learning point mentions postoperative hormonal suppression;
  the case description does not report that it was given, and it is not
  adopted. A retraction notice (PMID 42998648) has no abstract. The
  retracted 2015 paper (PMID 26319653) is not used in this repo, and the
  retracted claim is not summarized.
- **Mast-cell CCL2/CCR4 (PMID 42992652) is not an MRGPRX2 result.** The
  MRGPRX2 knowledge page is unchanged. REST (PMID 43013554) and HDAC3
  (PMID 42996627) are progesterone-signalling papers without a human
  treatment. The HDAC3 paper's 30%–50% infertility sentence is not
  adopted as a site figure.
